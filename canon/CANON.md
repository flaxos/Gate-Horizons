# Gate Horizons — Canon (Source of Truth)

## Purpose
Canon drift kills hard-sci settings. This file is the law. If something conflicts with CANON.md, the something loses.

## Design pillars (non-negotiable)
1) Hard-sci texture: spacecraft are fragile, budgets matter, timelines are slow, delta-v is king.
2) Gates are industrial infrastructure with constraints, not steerable magic.
3) Sol is a dead-end spur: one stable outbound link (at first).
4) Strategic timescale is months (travel to/from gates, charging cycles, build/crew/training, politics).
5) LLMs may generate content only inside guardrails: canon stability > cool ideas.

## Jump gate constraints (stable rules)
- Charging: requires time (days–weeks) and external power feed; each activation drains a finite reservoir.
- Windowing: opens in short stability windows (minutes–hours).
- Throughput: strict mass/energy budget per window.
- Addressing: destinations are network links, not free coordinates; Sol can only reach what it’s linked to.
- Placement: quiet gravity zones; gates avoid deep gravity wells for stability/safety.

## Aesthetic constraints (non-negotiable)
- No fantasy glow, no mystical beams, no alien runes/glyph spam.
- Human tech is utilitarian and slightly ugly: grime, patch plates, handholds, tethers, radiator farms.
- Gate artefacts are precise geometry, matte-black/dark iridescent, structural not decorative.

## Canon change control
Any change must document:
- what changes,
- why gameplay requires it,
- what it breaks/trades off,
- and bump a version:
