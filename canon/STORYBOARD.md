# Storyboard — Prequel (Numbered Frames)

## ACT I — The Moon That Lied
SB01 — Discovery control room: anomaly becomes undeniable in light curve + magnetometer.
SB02 — First flyby: dusty rubble moon; instruments show contradictions.
SB03 — Seismic "ringing": rubble pile behaves coherently; data unnerves the team.

## ACT II — The Seed Inside the Dust
SB04 — Tomography reveal: smooth torus cavity inside lumpy aggregate.
SB05 — First exposure: dust peeled back reveals matte-black engineered surface.
SB06 — Belt construction yard: ugly industrial build-out, drones, tugs, reactor modules.

## ACT III — Waking a Dead Machine
SB07 — Power injection attempt: spikes, harmonics, field collapse (controlled failure).
SB08 — 21 seconds: stable aperture appears as a sensor "absence".
SB09 — SPARROW-1 launch: micro-probe fired through; gate snaps shut.

## ACT IV — The Door Opens Both Ways (Sort of)
SB10 — Telemetry return: impossible starfield/spectra resolves into "other system".
SB11 — Crewed mission prep: gate-rated ship in dock, rugged and functional.
SB12 — Destination reveal: red star system, debris lattice, multiple dead/sealed gates.
