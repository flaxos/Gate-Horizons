# Style Bible — Gate Horizons (Anti-Drift)

## Visual tone
Hard sci-fi realism. Industrial, practical, believable. Documentary/35mm film still vibe. Minimal grading.

## Human tech (always)
- Tethers, handholds, EVA safety practice.
- Patch plates, sun-bleached insulation, taped harnessing, grime.
- Modular trusses, radiator farms, tug docks, maintenance scarring.
- UI: instrument-first, monochrome overlays, warning banners; no holograms.

## Dust Moon (HERMES-K)
- Lumpy rubble aggregate with electrostatic dust, slow shedding tails.
- Micro-impact scarring, craterlets stacked on craterlets.
- Interior scan reveals smooth manufactured curvature that nature shouldn't produce.

## Gate artefact
- Precise structural geometry; matte-black or dark iridescent material.
- No decorative glyphs, no runes, no "alien writing wallpaper".
- Built for stability, not beauty.

## Gate active look (later scenes)
- Aperture is a sharp "wrongness": an absence of expected sensor/camera behaviour.
- Not a swirling portal. Minimal VFX. Cameras struggle; instruments glitch.

## Forbidden drift
- No fantasy glow, no magical beams, no mysticism.
- No sleek Apple/Star Wars future.
- No EVA without tethers, no impossible gravity cheats.
