# Prequel Novella — "The Dust Moon Protocol" (Working Title)

## What this is
A hard-sci-fi prequel novella for the Gate Horizons universe. It covers humanity's discovery of HERMES-K — a dusty mini-moonlet in the asteroid belt that turns out to contain a dormant jump gate — and the years of scientific, political, and engineering effort that follow.

The novella grounds the Gate Horizons setting in believable, industrial-texture hard sci-fi before the game's strategic layer begins.
