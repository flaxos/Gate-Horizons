# Release Plan: Gameplay & Feature Growth

## Phase 1: Tactical Encounters + Diplomacy Foundation
**Scope**
- Deliver tactical hex combat mini-game with core actions and terrain effects.
- Introduce alien faction diplomacy basics (relations, choices, outcomes).
- Expand encounter narratives to support tactical and diplomatic paths.

**Success criteria**
- Players can opt into manual tactical resolution with meaningful choices.
- Diplomatic interactions influence encounter outcomes and faction standing.
- Encounter narratives branch cleanly for combat, diplomacy, and evasion.

**Dependencies / notes**
- Builds on engagement systems (orders, missions, encounter contracts).
- Requires narrative content updates and UI flow for tactical mode.

## Phase 2: Tech, Progression, and Procedural Exploration
**Scope**
- Complete the full tech tree (20–30 techs) with clear progression gates.
- Add procedural galaxy generation for replayable exploration.
- Expand event library and anomaly content for deeper discovery.

**Success criteria**
- Tech progression meaningfully changes ship/colony capabilities.
- Procedural galaxies feel balanced and varied across runs.
- Event library supports multiple tiers and discovery pacing.

**Dependencies / notes**
- Requires balancing passes for resource rates and encounter pacing.
- Leverages mission and gating systems from engagement phases.

## Phase 3: Expansion Features + Long-Run Goals
**Scope**
- Add new ship classes, resources, and encounter types.
- Introduce story campaign beats and achievement milestones.
- Improve session retention with long-term goals and unlocks.

**Success criteria**
- New content supports diverse play styles and strategic choices.
- Campaign beats and achievements provide clear mid/late-game goals.
- Retention loop encourages continued exploration and progression.

**Dependencies / notes**
- Depends on stable tactical/diplomacy systems and tech progression.
- Requires additional narrative content and balancing support.
